About Us

Building strength, crafting excellence.

Brown Bear Construction is a Minneapolis-based general contractor specializing in semi-custom to full-custom home construction, multifamily construction, renovations, and commercial spaces. We believe a home should be felt before it's explained. Walk through the door, and you should be able to picture your life unfolding there: coffee in the morning light of the back room, loved ones gathered around the table, the quiet moments that make a house a home.

Our homes are built with a timeless, classic aesthetic: clean lines, honest materials, and proportions that feel right for decades to come — the kind you pass down rather than remodel your way out of.
